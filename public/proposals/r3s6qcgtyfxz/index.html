03 · Being found on Google

Two Google setups, both included in the design fee.

A website is only useful if people can find it. These two setups are what make your business appear when someone searches for you, and they are included at no extra cost.

Google Search Console

A free tool from Google that tells Google your website exists and should be indexed. Once it is set up, your site can appear in search results when people search for your name, your business name or the services you offer.

It also lets us monitor how the site performs in search, see which searches bring people to you, and fix any issues Google flags.

Google Business Profile

The listing that appears on the right-hand side of Google and on Google Maps when someone searches for your business. It shows your contact details, location, trading hours and customer reviews, and links directly to your website.

Reviews on your profile build trust with new customers and help your business rank higher in local searches.

04 · How we work together

A simple process from first email to go-live.

I build the website from the content you provide, then we refine it together. The clearer the brief at the start, the faster and better the result.

  1. STEP 1

    You send me your content

    Email me everything you would like on the website. It does not need to be polished, I will shape it into the final pages. Ideally this includes:

    • About your business: who you are, what you do, who you serve, and what sets you apart.
    • What you want on the site: the pages you have in mind and what each should say.
    • Pictures: photos of your work, your premises, your team, and your logo if you have one.
    • Look and feel: colours you like, a theme or mood, and links to any websites or businesses whose style you admire.
    • Contact details: phone, email, address, trading hours and social media links.
  2. STEP 2

    I confirm the quote and register the domain

    With the page structure agreed, I confirm the exact once-off fee. We register your domain through domains.co.za and, if you want it, set up your email addresses.

  3. STEP 3

    I design and build the first version

    I build the full website based on your content and the style you described, and send you a private preview link to review on your phone and computer.

  4. STEP 4

    We fine-tune it together

    You tell me what you would like changed and I refine it. Up to two rounds of design revisions are included, which is usually more than enough to get it exactly right.

  5. STEP 5

    Go-live and Google setup

    Once you are happy, I publish the site on your domain, submit it to Google Search Console, and set up your Google Business Profile so you can start collecting reviews.
