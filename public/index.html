Xpanda Security sales platform

Sole developer · Xpanda Security (Argent Industrial Group) · Jan 2025 to 2026 · In production · Internal system, code under NDA

The problem

Xpanda manufactures and distributes security products nationally. Quoting, sales and ordering ran on paper and spreadsheets across dozens of branches. It was slow, prices drifted from the accounting system, and head office had no view of the pipeline. I was hired straight out of my degree to digitise the whole thing, on my own.

What I built

  • Kotlin Android tablet app covering the full quote, order and approval lifecycle for four user roles, with accounting-accurate pricing and role-gated status control.
  • Background PDF engine that generates retail, wholesale and order-summary documents silently, using chained rendering to avoid WebView contention.
  • Operations web portals for tracking, dispatch, invoicing, payments and KPIs, role-scoped with Firebase Auth, plus a customer-facing quote viewer with signature capture.
  • Cloud Functions backend that routes order emails by role, sends confirmations with conditional pricing panels, handles bulk email and rate-limited PIN recovery.

The result

The platform is complete and in daily use across the national branch network. I onboarded every branch myself, I handle after-hours incidents, and the architecture became the blueprint for the group's next rollout.

American Shutters sales platform

Sole developer · American Shutters (Argent Industrial Group) · 2026 to present · In development · Internal system, code under NDA

The problem

With Xpanda delivered, the group wants the same digital pipeline at American Shutters, a manufacturer of shutters and security barriers. Their products are configured rather than picked from a catalogue: measurements, finishes and options all change the price. And this time the brief is browser-only. No app store, nothing to install.

What I'm building

  • The proven platform pattern as a web app: the Firebase backend, Cloud Functions layer and operations portals that ran Xpanda, rebuilt browser-first.
  • A product and pricing engine reworked for configured products, where dimensions and finishes drive the quote instead of a fixed price list.
  • Role-based workflow across consultants, branch management and head office, with the same accounting-accurate pricing the group now expects.
  • Silent PDF generation for quotes and orders, built on the rendering approach proven in production on Xpanda.

Where it stands

In active development. The goal is a hardened, reusable foundation the group can roll out to further companies after this one.

How I work

The parts of the job that aren't code.

I talk to the people using it

Half of building useful software is working out what someone actually needs versus what they asked for. I've held that conversation with branch consultants, directors and everyone in between, and I onboarded all 28 Xpanda branches myself. If you want proof I can communicate, that rollout is it.

I own what I ship

When a production issue lands after hours, I'm at my machine within minutes. If it's critical I fix it there. If the system can keep running, I brief the people affected, give them a workable interim, and ship the proper fix in the morning. There's nobody to hand anything to, so I don't.

I use AI, and I answer for the output

I decide the architecture, data model and security boundaries, then use AI to move faster inside that plan. Every AI-assisted change gets read, understood and tested before it ships, because in production "the AI wrote it" is never an answer. I also ship AI in the product itself: ML Kit runs on-device in the Xpanda app.
