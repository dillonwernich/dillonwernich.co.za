How it works

A straightforward process from first conversation to go-live.

We talk about what you need

A short conversation about your business, what you want the website or app to do, and what you already have. From that I put together a clear proposal with a scope and a quote, so you know exactly what you are getting before anything starts.

You send me the content

Your text, pictures, logo, and any colours or websites you like the look of. It does not need to be polished. I shape it into the finished product.

I build it, then we refine it together

You get a private preview to review on your phone and computer. We go through it together, you tell me what to change, and I refine it until you are happy.

Go-live and after

I publish it, set up what is needed for people to find it, and hand over anything you need to run it. If you want changes or new features later, I am a message away and will always give you an estimate before I start.
